Preprint available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11442).

We develop a distance-weighted pressure norm and a boundary-distance-based pressure preconditioner for the Stokes equations. The analysis proves a Nečas-type inequality with a constant depending only on the dimension, and yields uniform norm equivalence for families of domains with bounded Hardy constants.
